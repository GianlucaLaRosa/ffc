# Footer and alerts

These settings **do not** live inside a single conference. They apply to the site.

## Footer

**Globals** menu → **Footer**.

Put institutional links here (**Structure**, **Delegation**). A link with a filled address **must** also have a **Label**. Addresses must start with `http://` or `https://`.

Use tab **Partners** for logos in the footer: **Image** (required), optional **URL**, and **Alt text**. Tab **Credits** is a list of **Role** and **Name**. Order in each list is the order on the site.

The same screen has tabs **Cookie Policy** and **Privacy Policy**. Those texts appear on `/cookie-policy` and `/privacy`. The footer always links there; **Title** is the label in the footer. If the body is empty after a first install, restart the app once: a default English text is loaded automatically. Do not leave the legal pages empty in production.

On first visit, the public site shows a short **cookie notice** at the bottom (technical storage only; no analytics or profiling). Participants must tick the checkbox and choose **Accept** once; the choice is remembered in local storage. Update the **Cookie Policy** tab if your legal team changes the wording—the banner links there.

## Programme alerts

**Globals** → **Programme alerts**.

This is for people who, on the site, save **talks or abstracts** (programme leaves) in “My programme” and ask for a reminder on the device.

In the panel **Start time** and **End time** are required. The reminder uses the start (lead time in **Minutes before start**) and the Live window until the end. If a previously saved item has no time, it stays in the list but **does not** generate alerts.

- **Enable session alerts** — if you untick it, the site hides the option and no alerts go out
- **Push contact email** — technical email (not shown to participants); required if alerts are on
- **Minutes before start** — lead time (1 to 30 minutes)
- **Notification title** — short name on the lock screen

Technical notification permissions are set by whoever administers the server. You only decide whether to enable them and with which text.

For **programme changes** during the conference (room, time) do not use this screen: use the edition **Notices** tab. See [Notices during the conference](/en/docs/avvisi-conferenza).

## Files and media

Images are uploaded from Logo, Photo, partner logos, and similar fields. They go into **Media**, in folders already prepared (logos, partner logos, people photos, abstract photos). You do not have to use the Media menu every day: the fields on the document are enough.
