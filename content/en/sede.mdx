# Venue and map

**Venue** tab of the conference.

## Required fields

- **City**
- **Country** — English is fine, e.g. `Italy`
- **Address** — for people who need to arrive

## Coordinates (recommended)

**Longitude** and **Latitude** drive the map. They are not an address: they are two numbers.

- Longitude = X (for Trieste, around `13.80`)
- Latitude = Y (for Trieste, around `45.65`)

You find them on Google Maps: right-click the point → the numbers appear in the menu. The **first** number Google shows is usually latitude, the **second** longitude: in the panel the order is reversed (longitude first, then latitude).

<Callout type="warning">
  If you swap the two numbers, the pin lands on another continent. Check the public page after publication.
</Callout>

## Location notes

**Location notes** is free text (entrance, room, accessibility). It is optional.

Do not copy the same sentences as the **Public listing** tab: that tab is for Google and link previews, not the map.
