# First login

<Steps>

### Open the panel

In the browser go to the site address and add `/admin` (example: `https://your-site.org/admin`).

Ask whoever runs the site for credentials. The login screen shows the **Fondazione per la Ricerca sulla Fibrosi Cistica - ETS** logo. After login you see the **Dashboard**. At the top there is a green **Publishing guide** box: open it for this handbook. If you are not signed in, you are sent to the login screen.

In the panel, pick **English** or **Italiano** from the language control. Field names stay in English in both languages.

### Look at the left menu

- **Edit active edition** (pencil, no group title) — opens the conference currently on the home page. Hidden if **Active conference** has no **Conference**
- **Site** — which edition is on the home page (**Active conference**) and **Conference archive**
- **Conferences** — list of all editions. Open an edition from here; for the one on the home page you can use **Edit active edition** at the top
- **People and organisations** — people and institutions, reused every year
- **Content** — files (**Media**) and users
- **References** — support lists (countries, regions, abstract statuses): you usually leave them alone
- **Globals** — footer (**Footer**) and **Programme alerts**

<Callout type="warning">
  Days, sessions, abstracts, and the appendix **do not** appear as separate menu items. They open **from inside** the conference, in the tabs at the top.
</Callout>

### Understand draft and published

Almost every document has two states:

- **Draft** — the public does not see it yet (except preview).
- **Published** — if the edition is active or in the archive, the content can appear on the site.

At the top right are the buttons to save (**Save**, **Save Draft**) and to publish (**Publish changes**). Save often: the system also keeps a history.

### Use preview

On the conference document you can open a preview of the intro, even while it is still a draft. Use it to check copy and blocks **before** you publish.

</Steps>

## Tip

Create the **people** and **institutions** you will reuse first. Then create the edition and, from there, days and programme. That way you do not stop work to “go find a speaker”.
