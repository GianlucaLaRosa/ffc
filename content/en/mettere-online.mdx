# Publish and archive

Two distinct ideas:

1. **Published** on the document — “this content is ready”
2. **Active conference** — “this edition is the site home”

You can have many published editions. **Only one** is on the home page.

<Steps>

### Publish the pieces of the edition

To show a complete programme, publish in a chain:

1. the **conference**
2. each **day**
3. each **session** (and children)
4. the **abstracts** that must be visible
5. the **appendix**, if there is one

A published conference with days still in draft shows little or nothing in that section.

### Put the edition on the home page

**Site** menu (or Dashboard) → **Active conference**.

Choose a conference that is **already published**. Save.

That edition:

- appears at the main address `/`
- **leaves** the public archive on its own, if it was there

<Callout type="warning">
  Do not leave an old edition on the home page by mistake. Check the title on **Edit active edition** (top of the left menu, or Dashboard).
</Callout>

### Archive past editions

**Conference archive** (still under **Site**). At the top, **Enable public archive** shows or hides **Archive** in the site menu. It saves as soon as you tick it (no **Save**).

- **On:** **Archive** appears in the site menu if at least one edition is ticked below.
- **Off:** **Archive** is hidden in the menu. `/archive` (the list) and `/archive/{slug}` (each edition) stay available if someone has the link. The ticks on individual editions are kept.

Then decide which published editions (not the active one) appear in the list. A ticked row is public at `/archive/` plus that conference’s **Slug**.

The active edition is **not** in the archive: if someone opens the old archive link, they are sent home.

From `/archive` (the list) the menu has **Current edition** (and **Archive** if **Enable public archive** is on), without Programme / Venue / Appendix: those sections do not exist on the list. On a past edition page (`/archive/` + slug) Programme / Venue / Appendix stay, and they belong to **that** programme. A bar under the header reminds you this is not the home edition and offers **Open current edition**. The **logo** always opens the home page.

### Card for Google and social

**Public listing** tab of the conference. Help text there follows the panel language; field names stay in English.

- **Result title**, **Short listing text**, **Preview image** — title, two sentences, image when the link is forwarded
- **Facts for AI answers** — optional, for names and short facts. Do not repeat city and dates already in **Venue** and **Days**

If you are unsure, leave them empty: the site uses name, venue, and dates.

</Steps>
